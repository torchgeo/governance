## How to Join

All technical steering committee meetings are open to the public, and all members of the TorchGeo community are invited to attend. However, only the above members are granted a vote on official business. Community members who have made substantial contributions to the TorchGeo Organization are eligible to become official TSC members. Contributions come in many forms:

- **Code contributions**: individuals who serve as a maintainer for a project that belongs to the TorchGeo Organization
- **Financial contributions**: organizations who make substantial donations or sponsorships to the TorchGeo Organization
- **Organizational contributions**: individuals who serve as liaisons or volunteer to lead efforts for the TorchGeo Organization

Organizations are typically limited to 1 or 2 seats on the TSC to avoid any one group having too much power and to retain independence. If you or your organization satisfies one or more of these criteria, we will reach out to you to invite you to join the TSC.
